# Echo Escape 걷기 스프라이트

`walk_atlas.png`는 본체와 분신 원본을 참조해 내장 이미지 생성 도구로 만든 걷기 동작 시트다. 정지 상태는 `player.png`와 `echo.png`를 그대로 사용한다. `walk_frames.json`은 각 자세의 왼쪽, 위쪽, 폭, 높이를 픽셀 단위로 지정한다.

## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one game sprite atlas with EIGHT distinct full-body movement frames for the EXACT Echo Escape boy and his identical cyan spectral echo in the references. Reference image 1 is the definitive physical boy, dark tousled hair, black/navy short mantle with ochre edging and clasp, dark trousers and brown tall boots, painted fantasy art. Reference image 2 is the definitive cyan translucent version of the same boy. Preserve the costume, face, proportions, and rendering as closely as possible. ONE transparent 4-column by 2-row regular grid, 8 equally sized cells with generous gaps and no overlaps. Top row: 4 physical boy side-facing-RIGHT brisk WALK cycle frames left to right. Bottom row: precisely matched walk poses but with the cyan spectral echo appearance. All eight face right, similar body scale and center, their boots aligned to each row's baseline. Pose 1: near leg reaching forward, far leg extended back, opposite arms swing. Pose 2: feet pass beneath hips, near knee bent, torso over the standing foot. Pose 3: FAR leg reaching forward, near leg extended backward, arms reverse, same body orientation. Pose 4: opposite passing pose, far knee bent. Show visible, distinct arm and knee positions in every frame, full boots and cape uncropped. Regular grid with clear horizontal and vertical TRANSPARENT gutters so each cell can be extracted as one sprite. Real alpha transparent background, no ground, no shadows, no lettering, no dividing lines, no labels, no decoration, no particles between characters. Same clean hand-painted sprite aesthetic as both references. Wide landscape atlas approximately 2:1, with exactly eight separated sprites.
